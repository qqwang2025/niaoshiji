鳥事記重視你的隱私。本政策說明我們如何處理你使用本 App 時產生的資訊。

### 這個 App 是誰營運的？

### 我們收集哪些資料？

| 資料 | 用途 |
|------|------|
| **事件紀錄**（時間、名稱、emoji、備註） | 顯示日曆與統計分析，儲存在你的裝置上 |
| **自訂事件庫** | 供你快速記錄，儲存在你的裝置上 |
| **語言偏好** | 記住你選擇的顯示語言 |

我們不會收集你的位置、通訊錄或財務資訊；App 目前也不需要註冊帳號。

### AI 功能會用到哪些資料？
使用「AI 農民曆」或「AI 總結」時，你的事件文字會透過我們的中介伺服器（Cloudflare Workers）傳送給 **Google Gemini API** 以產生分析內容。中介伺服器僅轉發請求與回應，**不會儲存或記錄**你傳送的內容，也不會用於訓練 AI 模型。我們不會出售你的任何資料，僅在法律要求時才會依法揭露必要資訊。

### 資料保留與刪除
你可以隨時在 App 內刪除個別紀錄，或解除安裝 App 以完整移除所有資料。

### 政策異動
若我們的資料處理方式有變動（例如未來加入跨裝置同步功能），會在此更新政策內容；持續使用本服務即視為同意更新後的版本。

## English

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how 鳥事記 handles information generated while you use the app.

### Who Operates This App?
Operated by an independent developer. Contact: qqwang2025@gmail.com

### What Data Do We Collect?

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Event records** (timestamp, name, emoji, note) | Powers the calendar and analysis views, stored on your device |
| **Custom event library** | Lets you log quickly next time, stored on your device |
| **Language preference** | Remembers your chosen display language |

We do not collect your location, contacts, or financial information. The app currently requires no account.

### What Data Does the AI Feature Use?
When you use the "AI Almanac" or "AI Summary" features, your event text is sent through our relay server (Cloudflare Workers) to **Google Gemini API** to generate the analysis. Our relay only forwards the request and response — it does **not** store or log your content, and content is not used to train AI models. We do not sell your data, and only disclose information when required by law.

### Data Retention & Deletion
Delete individual records anytime within the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

### Changes to This Policy
If how we handle data changes (for example, adding cross-device sync in the future), we'll update this policy here. Continued use after changes means you accept the update.
